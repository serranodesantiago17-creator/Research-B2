# Student's Book audio (24 tracks)

The tracks were generated with **Kokoro TTS** (open-source, Apache 2.0 licence) from the audio scripts in the Teacher's Guide. Each speaker has a voice close to their accent. Some accents (Nigerian, Kenyan, German, Irish, Māori New Zealand, Jordanian, Korean, Bulgarian, Australian and South African) are approximate, because no free voices exist for them; for these speakers a clear British, European or East Asian voice was used. Every track starts with a short announcement and was checked with automatic speech recognition against its script.

The site finds these files automatically. To replace a track with a real recording, upload an MP3 with the same name (64–96 kbps mono is enough for speech), or paste a link next to the track in `js/audio-config.js` (links take priority over files).
